## Design Context

### Users
People tracking their own bloodwork over time — health-conscious adults, patients
managing a condition, and families keeping several profiles. They arrive with a
PDF or a printout of lab results (which they type in) and want to understand a
trend, not decode a table. Context is calm and considered, not urgent: reviewing history at home,
before or after a doctor's visit.

### Brand Personality
**Calm, clear, trustworthy.** Voice is plain and human — "your lab's range,
not a textbook one," never clinical jargon or hype. It should feel like a
well-kept personal record: everything legible, nothing shouting. Evokes clarity
and quiet confidence, never alarm.

### Aesthetic Direction
**"Clear chart", warmed up: a health record you can read at arm's length, set
on painted green-to-orange artwork.** References the user chose: Superpower
(warm media cards, numbered steps with frosted UI over imagery, pill buttons)
and split-hero SaaS pages with a painterly gradient panel and a floating glass
product card. Product-led, not agency-editorial: every marketing preview is the
real app component over sample data, and the same screens are public at
`/demo`.

- **One gradient family only: evergreen into orange.** It appears as (1) the
  painted artwork in `public/art` (wind-swept oil-paint panels, generated by
  `scripts/art`), (2) soft corner washes (`.card-wash`, the page background),
  and (3) small brand accents (`--grad-brand`: logo tile, active avatar, the
  one gradient phrase in the hero). Never introduce a second hue pair
  (no purple/blue/pink meshes), and avoid large flat colour blocks — a big
  surface is either paper, a wash, or artwork.
- **Frosted glass on artwork.** Product UI floats on art as `.glass` (light,
  ink text) or `.glass-dark` (white text). White type directly on art needs
  the green part of the painting behind it (`flip`) and/or `scrim`. Soft
  floating shadow (`--shadow-float`) is allowed on glass only; ordinary cards
  stay flat on hairlines.
- **Colour roles.** Paper canvas `#F5F4EF`; evergreen-black ink `#13231F`;
  interaction evergreen `#0F5C4D`; warm orange `#EE7420` for brand surfaces
  only. **Status colours stay reserved for data** — in range `#0A8F6C`,
  borderline `#D07C00`, out `#D1345B` (validated for colour-blind separation
  and ≥3:1 on white) — and status is **never colour alone**: each state has
  its own shape (dot / diamond / arrow up or down / hollow ring) and a plain
  label. Brand orange is never used for a data mark (amber "borderline" is a
  status; orange is not), and charts and tables sit on plain white or glass,
  not on a wash.
- **Type.** One grotesque (Google Sans): display headings at **weight 500
  max, sentence case**, often two-tone (ink, then `text-ink-3` for the second
  phrase). **All UI text — nav, buttons, labels — is sentence-case sans, 12px
  minimum.** JetBrains Mono only for the small uppercase section kicker
  (`.eyebrow`) and the unit beside a reading (`.unit`).
- **Shape.** Pill buttons and nav items (`rounded-full`); 16–20px cards; 24–28px
  art panels and hero cards. Typeset punctuation: en-dashes in ranges,
  `10³/µL` superscripts (`prettyUnit`), tabular numerals in columns.
- **Motion.** One easing curve, `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`; nothing
  bounces. The artwork is static. Motion shows what the product does: on the
  landing page, parts play once in view inside `<Reveal anim>` (`.m-anim`) —
  `.m-item` fade-up, `.m-pop`, `.m-draw` (a line drawing), `.m-grow` (a bar
  filling), range-bar dots sliding to their value, `CountUp` figures — each
  staggered with `--d`. Copy stays short: a headline, then at most one line.

**Anti-references:** a second gradient palette, AI-purple meshes, flat
single-colour hero blocks, serif or bold display headlines, decorative use of
status colours, uppercase-mono buttons and nav, tiny (<12px) labels, pure
`#000`/`#fff` page surfaces, stock photos of people or invented customer
logos and testimonials, fake browser chrome or phone mock-ups, marquees,
parallax, health "scores" or alarm language.

### Design Principles
1. **Show the product, with real components, on the artwork.** Landing
   previews, the auth side panel and `/demo` all render the app's own
   components over `lib/demo` — never a drawn mock-up — floated as glass on a
   painted panel.
2. **Status is the only colour, and never alone.** Green/amber/red appear on
   results only, always with the status shape and a label. Chart text wears
   ink tokens, never the status colour.
3. **Answer "how am I doing?" first.** Overview leads with one figure (N of M
   in range), then only what needs a closer look as cards, then everything
   else folded into body systems. Detail leads with the reading, then the
   line, then the same data as a table.
4. **One line per marker.** Reference range as a shaded band, each test a
   status-shaped point, life events as labelled rules, a legend under the
   plot. Pointing or arrow keys move the reading block to that test.
5. **Plain words, honest claims.** "In range / Borderline / High / Low", "your
   lab's range, not a textbook one". Marketing copy states only what the
   product does today (no PDF import, no diagnosis).
6. **Quiet, single-curve motion.** Fade-up on entry (once), lines that
   draw, bars that fill, numbers that count up, 0.25–0.3s hovers. Motion is armed only under
   `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` *and* `scripting: enabled`, so
   content is never hidden from anyone.

> Tokens and component classes (`.card`, `.card-wash`, `.art`, `.glass`,
> `.glass-dark`, `.chip`, `.seg`, `.eyebrow`, `.acc`, `.reveal`) live in
> `src/app/globals.css` — tokens on `:root` (a `[data-theme="dark"]` block is
> kept for dark surfaces), component classes inside `@layer components` so
> utilities win. Artwork: `<Art name=…>` in `src/components/ui/art.tsx`
> (`ribbed`, `scrim`, `flip`), images in `public/art`.
> Landing: `src/app/page.tsx` (+ `src/components/landing/`). Product screens
> are shared views in `src/components/views/` rendered by both `/app` (real
> data, editable) and `/demo` (sample data, read-only) via `ViewCtx`; the
> frame is `src/components/app/app-shell.tsx` (sidebar on desktop, top bar +
> tab bar on mobile). Sample data: `src/lib/demo.ts`.
